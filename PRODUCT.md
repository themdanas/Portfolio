# Product

**What it is:** the personal portfolio of MD Anas, an AI engineer and Computer Science student building LLM systems: inference servers, agents, training pipelines, and RAG.

**Register:** brand. The site is the work sample, so the design is the product.

**Audience:** recruiters and ML/infra engineers. They usually read on a laptop between other tabs, or on a phone from a LinkedIn link. They want to know what he built, how deep it goes, and how to reach him, all in under a minute.

**Voice:** precise, quiet, candid. Claims are specific and measured, never promotional.

**Anti-references:**
- SaaS landing-page cards and hero metrics.
- The editorial-serif magazine lane.
- Terminal or hacker costume: green-on-black, fake prompts.

**Constraints:**
- Minimal motion.
- Fast static pages.
- Readable long-form technical writing: notes and case studies, with code, tables, and diagrams.
